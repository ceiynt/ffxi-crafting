CatsEyeXI Synthesis Mechanics, Caps & Level 100 Rewards

Verified custom synthesis mechanics, subcraft bonuses, custom HELM gathering unlocks, and Level 100 Kupo Shield / Relic Weapon Voucher turn-ins on CatsEyeXI.

Skill Caps & Multipliers

  • Main Craft Cap: 110 (1 Guild at 110; Crystal Warrior can reach 110 in all).
  • Sub-Craft Cap: 70 in all other guilds.
  • +11 Over-Cap Rule: You can continue gaining skill-ups up to +11 levels above any recipe's natural skill cap!
  • Skill-Up Rate: 1.5x retail rate | Multiplier: 3.0x amount.
  • Fishing Skill-Up Rate: 3.0x retail rate.
  • No Crafting Myths: Compass directions, moon phase, and day of the week do not alter HQ or break rates.

All Subcrafts at 70 Bonus

Leveling your remaining 7 sub-crafts to the 70 cap provides a permanent passive synthesis bonus across all recipes:

-24% Material Loss Rate
+8% Synthesis Success

Guild Points: 2.5x multiplier, 10,000 GP daily cap (resets JP Midnight).

CatsEyeXI Custom HELM Unlocks

  • Gustav Tunnel Mining: Complete Broken Bones (Khartes in Selbina, Lv.60) to mine Iron, Steel, Mythril, Darksteel & Adaman Ore.
  • Ifrit's Cauldron Gems: Complete Rock Cooking (Smolderoq at E-5, Lv.50, 12x Beastcoins) to appraise Igneous Rocks into Garnet, Peridot, Sphene, Turquoise, Obsidian & Silver Ore.
  • Bibiki Bay Logging: Custom logging points yield Elm, Chestnut, Rosewood, Mahogany, Ebony & Petrified Logs.
